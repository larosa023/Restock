## Restock 0.1.0

The first release. Restock keeps every app on your PC current with one click, except your larosa023 apps, which Hangar handles.

- **Updates in one place:** apps from winget and from Windows' own Apps & features list, with icon, installed and available version, and source.
- **Update all** (Ctrl+U) or one at a time. Updates run in a queue, silently where the installer allows, with progress and a result for each app.
- **Open apps are handled for you:** Restock asks, closes the app the normal way, updates it, and opens it again.
- **Administrator rights only when needed:** Windows asks for one update at a time, never at launch.
- **Skip a version, pin an app, or ignore it.** Self-updating apps (Chrome, Discord, Steam…) are left to their own updaters unless you include them.
- **Schedule from the tray:** check daily or weekly, get a notification when updates are waiting, and optionally update everything overnight.
- **History** of every update with the date and the versions before and after.
- 14 themes, accent colors and custom theme files in the Hangar format. Restock updates itself.
